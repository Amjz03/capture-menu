How it works

  1. 01

    Play

    It quietly keeps the last 30 minutes of your game, on any screen, with any graphics card.

  2. 02

    Press

    Once for the menu over your game: save 15 seconds up to 30 minutes, screenshot or record. Twice to save the last 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Keep

    Every game gets its own folder. Watch it, trim it to the frame, share it to Discord or your phone.

Everything after the press

Saving is one part. The rest of the app is built for what you do next with the clip, with a controller or a mouse.

Everything it does

Everything Capture Menu does, in one place.

The last 15 seconds to 30 minutes
It keeps the last 30 minutes of any game. Save 15 or 30 seconds, 1, 3, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or all of it.
Press twice, it's saved
Alt + C or Create twice saves the last 30 seconds, without opening anything.
Screenshots and recordings
From the same bar. A small REC timer shows while you record, for as long as you like.
Every controller
DualSense, Xbox and most others. The buttons on screen match the controller in your hands.
A folder for every game
Found by itself, like the PS5. Clips and screenshots can go to different drives.
Watch and trim to the frame
Open a clip right away, skip through it and cut it exactly where you want. The original stays.
Your voice on its own track
Make your voice louder or quieter in any clip afterwards, and change it back whenever you like.
Share in one step
A copy sized for your phone or Discord (10, 50 or 500 MB) goes straight to the clipboard.
Follows your game
Across monitors and TVs, and it never shows up in your own recordings.
Nothing leaves your PC
It only talks to OBS on your PC, and to GitHub when you check for updates. Your clips stay with you.
English and Arabic
The whole interface, right to left in Arabic, like the PS5.
Free, with no account
Open source under the MIT license. No ads and no watermark.

Install it in a minute

Windows 10 or 11. No admin rights needed.

  1. 01

    Download

    Get CaptureMenu-Setup.exe, about 50 MB.

  2. 02

    If your browser warns you, keep it

    Capture Menu isn't signed yet, so browsers don't know it. Windows Defender finds nothing in it. Chrome: open Downloads and press Keep. Edge: press ··· next to the file, then Keep, then Keep anyway.

  3. 03

    If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

    Press More info, then Run anyway.

  4. 04

    Press Install

    Setup installs OBS Studio and FFmpeg if you don't have them and sets everything up for your PC. A minute later, press Alt + C in a game.
