How it works

  1. 01

    Play

    It quietly keeps the last 30 minutes of your game, on any screen, with any graphics card.

  2. 02

    Press

    Once for the menu over your game, to save, take a screenshot or record. Twice to save the last 30 seconds.

  3. 03

    Keep

    Every clip lands in its game's folder, ready to watch, trim and share.

Everything after the press

Saving is one part. The rest of the app is built for what you do next with the clip, with a controller or a mouse.

Questions

Will it slow my game down?

OBS records on your graphics card, and the last minutes are kept on your drive, not in RAM. Capture Menu itself waits quietly in the tray.

Which controllers work?

DualSense, Xbox and most others, plus Alt + C on the keyboard. The buttons on screen match the controller in your hands.

Where do my clips go?

A folder for every game, found by itself. Clips and screenshots can go to different drives, and recording follows your game across monitors and TVs.

Is my voice recorded?

If you want. It goes on its own track, so you can make it louder or quieter in any clip later, or leave it out of a copy you share.

How do I share a clip?

Pick Phone & WhatsApp, or Discord at 10, 50 or 500 MB. A copy that fits is made and copied, ready to paste with Ctrl + V.

Why does my browser or Windows warn me?

Capture Menu isn't code-signed yet, so Windows can't show a verified publisher. Download it only from the official release on GitHub and follow the install steps below. A clean virus scan is a good sign, not a guarantee.

Does anything leave my PC?

Your clips and settings aren't sent anywhere. It talks to OBS on your PC, and to GitHub only when you check for updates. This website counts visits with GoatCounter, without cookies.

Is it really free?

Yes. Open source under the MIT license, with no account, no ads and no watermark. In English and Arabic.

Install it in a minute

Windows 10 or 11. No admin rights needed.

  1. 01

    Download

    Get CaptureMenu-Setup.exe, about 50 MB.

  2. 02

    If your browser warns you

    Capture Menu isn't code-signed yet, so browsers don't recognise it and may warn you. Keep the file only if it came from the official release page. Chrome: open Downloads and press Keep. Edge: press ··· next to the file, then Keep, then Keep anyway.

  3. 03

    If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”

    Without a signature, Windows shows the publisher as unknown. If the file came from the official release, press More info, then Run anyway.

  4. 04

    Press Install

    Setup installs OBS Studio and FFmpeg if you don't have them and sets everything up for your PC. A minute later, press Alt + C in a game.
